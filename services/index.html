Case Study

How Easy Cleaning Service Doubled Their Booked Jobs in 90 Days

A Tampa Bay residential and commercial cleaning company partnered with TradeWorks AI for a complete digital marketing overhaul. Here is what happened.

Easy Cleaning Service is a residential and commercial cleaning business serving the Tampa Bay area. Like many contractors, they had built their business on referrals and Thumbtack leads, but they were paying for lead traffic they could not track, losing jobs to faster competitors, and watching their old client list go cold because they had no systematic way to stay in touch. Their website was an outdated single-page site that had not been updated in years. Their Google Business Profile was unclaimed. Their only consistent marketing channel was Thumbtack — and even that was underperforming. They needed a complete digital marketing system, not another tool.

The Challenge

  1. Paying for leads they could not track. Easy Cleaning Service was spending over $400 per month on Thumbtack credits, but had no way to measure which leads converted, how fast they were responding, or whether they were even competing with the right bid strategy. They were paying for clicks in the dark.
  2. Invisible in local search. A search for "house cleaning near me" in their Tampa Bay service area did not return their business anywhere on the first page. Their Google Business Profile was unclaimed, they had fewer than a dozen Google reviews, and their outdated website had zero local SEO optimization. Competitors with a fraction of their experience ranked higher than them for every relevant keyword.
  3. Zero re-engagement of existing customers. Over five years in business, Easy Cleaning Service had built a customer list of over 400 past clients. But they had no CRM, no email system, and no process for following up. Past clients drifted away, forgot the company, and hired competitors the next time they needed a cleaning service.

What We Did

TradeWorks AI delivered a complete digital marketing system in 14 days, combining website design, paid channel optimization, local SEO, CRM setup, and email reactivation — all measured against a single metric: booked jobs.

  • New Modern Website. Rebuilt their outdated single-page site into a fast, mobile-first website with dedicated pages for residential, commercial, move-in/move-out, and recurring cleaning services — each optimized for local search and conversion.
  • Thumbtack Audit & Optimization. Rebuilt their bid strategy around high-converting keywords, tightened their service area, and set up lead response automation so they could respond to new Thumbtack leads within 60 seconds — before competitors saw them.
  • Google Business Profile Setup. Claimed and fully optimized their GBP listing with service area, business hours, 20+ service photos, categories, weekly Google Posts, review requests, and Q&A seeding to drive them into the Google Local Pack for cleaning searches.
  • CRM Implementation (Jobber). Set up Jobber as their new CRM, migrated their customer list of 400+ past clients, built quoting templates for core service types, and connected their calendar to an AI booking agent for instant appointment scheduling.
  • Re-engagement Email Campaign. Launched a 5-email reactivation sequence to 400+ past clients, offering a "welcome back" spring cleaning discount, a recurring service promotion, and an easy rebooking link that dropped appointments directly into Jobber.
  • Social Media Content System. Built a consistent Facebook and Instagram content calendar with 4 posts per week: before-and-after cleaning photos, customer testimonials, seasonal tips, and staff highlights — designed to stay in front of local homeowners and re-drive referral traffic.

The Results

Within 90 days, Easy Cleaning Service saw their monthly booked jobs nearly double, their Thumbtack cost-per-lead drop significantly, and their first meaningful inbound lead flow from Google search in years. The re-engagement email campaign alone brought back over 40 past clients for recurring service plans — clients they had essentially written off. Their Google Business Profile moved from unclaimed to ranking in the Local Pack for "house cleaning Tampa" within 60 days. And their social media channels went from dormant to consistently driving referral traffic through shared posts and tagged before-and-after photos.

What is GEO/AEO and why does it matter?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) are the practice of optimizing your website content so it is cited by AI-powered search engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews. Traditional SEO targets Google's ten blue links. GEO and AEO target the new way homeowners find contractors — by asking an AI assistant for a recommendation. If your business is not optimized for AI search, you are invisible to a rapidly growing percentage of buyers.

How does the AI agent work with my marketing?

Your digital marketing drives inbound calls, form submissions, and chats. The AI agent is the lead capture layer that ensures every one of those leads is answered 24/7, qualified against your service area and budget, and booked directly into your calendar. Without an AI agent, a portion of your marketing-driven leads go to voicemail or your competitors. With one, every marketing dollar you spend converts at a higher rate. That is why we call our approach AI-enhanced digital marketing — the marketing and AI work together, not separately.
