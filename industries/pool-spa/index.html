Two demand sources, one membership

Your single pool and spa membership is fed by two different kinds of customer, and both bring the recurring-route work that makes this trade so valuable.

Homeowners - The classic residential mix - recurring weekly and monthly pool service (the backbone), chemical balancing, green-pool recovery, equipment repair, resurfacing, and hot tub and spa service. Florida's pool density and year-round swimming make recurring service enormous, with a summer surge in repairs and green recovery. They find you through the marketplace, pick you on your pricing and rating, and - because it's a weekly relationship - stay with you for years.

Commercial, HOA & property managers - This is the crown jewel of the trade: recurring commercial and community pool contracts. Condo and apartment pools, HOA amenity pools, and hotel and gym pools all need scheduled service - and public and commercial pools require a Certified Pool Operator and health-department compliance, which raises the bar and rewards qualified companies. Property managers book against written service levels (routine within 48 hours, urgent within 24, emergency within 4). A single commercial or HOA pool contract can be worth many residential accounts, and it renews year after year.

Do I need a license to join as a pool company?

It depends on the work. Routine pool cleaning isn't state-licensed in Florida, but repairs, equipment, and resurfacing require a DBPR pool contractor or servicing license, and commercial or public pools require a Certified Pool Operator. We verify the credential that matches your services, plus $1M/$2M general liability naming TradeWorks AI as additional insured, workers' comp if you have employees, and a current W-9.
