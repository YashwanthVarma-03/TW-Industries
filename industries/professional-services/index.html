The Challenge

The Professional Services Firm Challenge

Missed Prospect Calls

The average firm misses 30% of inbound calls. Every unanswered call is a new client choosing another firm — and a single engagement’s lifetime value can exceed $25,000. Reception is juggling check-ins, conflict checks, and rescheduling while the phone rings.

No-Shows and Follow-Up Gaps

Missed consultations cost firms thousands per month in lost revenue and wasted attorney or advisor time. Without automated re-engagement campaigns, past clients due for annual reviews, tax prep, or periodic consultations fall through the cracks — hurting both relationships and revenue.

Staff Overwhelmed with Admin

Reception and paralegal teams spend hours on repetitive tasks — running conflict checks, confirming appointments, returning prospect calls, and managing intake paperwork. This administrative burden leads to burnout, errors, and a poor first impression for high-value prospects.

A new prospect calls your firm at 7 PM after seeing your firm online. The AI agent answers immediately, collects their name, matter or service type, urgency, and preferred consultation time. It books the consultation directly into your calendar, sends a confirmation text with intake form links, and logs the new prospect in your system — all with confidentiality-aware data handling. Consultation Rescheduling

A client texts your firm to reschedule their Thursday consultation. The AI agent checks availability, offers three alternative time slots, confirms the new appointment, and sends an updated confirmation — all without your staff lifting a finger. If the cancelled slot opens up a prime time, the AI can offer it to prospects on your waitlist automatically.

Client Re-Engagement Follow-Up

Your firm has 200 past clients due for annual reviews, tax-season check-ins, or periodic consultations. The AI agent calls, texts, or emails each client based on their communication preference, reminds them they are due for a touchpoint, and offers available slots. Clients who respond are booked immediately. Those who do not respond receive a follow-up at your configured interval — without adding staff hours.

Engagement & Scope Inquiry

A prospect calls asking whether your firm handles a specific matter or service line. The AI agent checks against your practice areas, confirms fit, and if it is a match, offers to schedule an initial consultation right away. For inquiries outside your scope, the AI politely captures the details and flags the lead for your intake team to review or refer — with confidentiality-aware handling throughout.
