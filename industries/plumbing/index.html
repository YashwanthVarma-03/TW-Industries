What a confirmed Plumbing work order looks like

"Confirmed work order" is not marketing language - it is a specific thing. Here is a real one, start to finish.

A homeowner in your ZIP wakes up Saturday to a cold shower and a puddle spreading across the garage floor - the water heater gave out overnight. She opens the marketplace, filters to Plumbing, sees your company with an upfront diagnostic price and a 4.9 rating, and books the first open slot: Saturday, 9 to 11 AM. She approves the diagnostic when she books.

The order drops into your queue already complete: the service (water heater repair or maintenance), the agreed diagnostic, the address, the two-hour window, and a note that the unit is in the garage and the main shutoff is on the north wall. You accept it. No one else in your ZIP can take it.

Two demand sources, one membership

Your single plumbing membership is fed by two different kinds of customer, and the second one is the reason members treat Select as a volume channel rather than an occasional job.

Homeowners - The classic residential mix - burst-pipe and sewer-backup emergencies, clogged drains, failed water heaters, leaking fixtures, and the four- and five-figure repipe and sewer-line decisions that follow. They find you through the marketplace, pick you on your pricing and rating, and book directly.

Property managers - This is the volume most lead platforms cannot give you - and plumbing is one of the trades property managers lean on hardest. They run dozens of doors - single-family rentals, small multifamily, HOAs - and every one has a water heater on the clock, a disposal that will jam, and a shutoff a tenant will call about. Through TradeWorks they book against written service levels (routine within 48 hours, urgent within 24, emergency within 4) and come back constantly: leak repairs, water-heater swaps, drain maintenance, and turnover fixture work across a whole portfolio. For a plumbing company, that is steady, repeatable revenue between the emergency spikes.

Plumbing contractor questions

How do plumbing contractors get work orders through TradeWorks Select?

Apply once and get vetted. After approval, property managers and homeowners in your ZIP browse the network, see upfront pricing, and book plumbing service on your calendar. The order arrives confirmed - scope, schedule, and access already set - and you are the only contractor on it.

How much does a TradeWorks Select plumbing membership cost?

One flat monthly membership, priced to your ZIP's market. Across plumbing in Florida the median is around $100 per month, but your exact figure depends on your ZIP - enter it in the lookup on this page to see your number. There are no per-job fees, no percentages, and no cost when a job cancels. It is introductory launch pricing and early members lock in their rate.

Why isn't the price a flat national number?

Because a ZIP's demand, home values, and cost to serve are not the same everywhere, a single national price would overcharge some contractors and undercharge others. TradeWorks prices each ZIP to its own market from a statewide model, so the number is fair and defensible - and you can see it before you apply.

Is this a plumbing lead generation service?

No. Lead services sell the same homeowner's information to several contractors who then compete for the job. Select sends confirmed work orders that a customer has already booked - one contractor per order, no bidding, no shared leads.

What's the best Thumbtack, Angi, or HomeAdvisor alternative for plumbers?

TradeWorks Select. Those platforms charge per shared lead whether or not it converts, and hand the same homeowner to several plumbers at once. Select charges one flat membership, every order is already booked, and you're the only contractor on it. You keep 100% of the invoice, and a cancelled order costs you nothing extra.

How can I get plumbing jobs without buying shared leads?

Join a network that sends confirmed work instead of selling contact information. With TradeWorks Select, homeowners and property managers book you directly through the marketplace, so every job in your queue is already scoped and scheduled - nothing to chase, no per-lead fees, and one flat monthly membership as your only cost.

What license and insurance does a plumbing contractor need to join?

An active state plumbing license (DBPR CFC in Florida), $1M/$2M general liability naming TradeWorks AI as additional insured, workers' comp if you have employees, backflow certification where the work requires it, and a current W-9.

How many plumbing contractors do you accept per ZIP code?

Seats per ZIP are deliberately limited to protect work-order volume for the members already in the network. The exact number a ZIP supports is shown in the lookup on this page. Once a ZIP is full, applications for it close until a seat opens.
