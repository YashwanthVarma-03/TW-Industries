What a confirmed Painting work order looks like

"Confirmed work order" is not marketing language - it is a specific thing, and for painting it means an estimate shared with you and at most one other pro, not a five-way race. Here is a real one, start to finish.

A homeowner in your ZIP is ready to repaint the exterior of her house before she lists it. She opens the marketplace, filters to Painting, and requests an exterior painting quote. Because it is a larger project, it routes as a quote request to your company and at most one other vetted painter - not blasted to five.

The request arrives with real detail: two-story, stucco, roughly 2,400 square feet, wants a color change from beige to white, hoping to start in two weeks. You review it, price it accurately, and submit your quote through the platform - no phone tag, no racing to call back first.

Two demand sources, one membership

Your single painting membership is fed by two different kinds of customer, and the second one is the steadiest, highest-volume work in the entire trade.

Homeowners - The classic residential mix - interior and exterior repaints, cabinet refinishing, accent walls, fence painting, wallpaper and popcorn-ceiling removal, and the pre-sale and post-move projects that come with buying or selling a home. They find you through the marketplace, pick you on your pricing and reviews, and book directly - the occasional single room, and the five-figure whole-house repaints.

Property managers - This is the volume most lead platforms cannot give you, and for painting it is enormous: turnover painting. Every time a tenant moves out, the unit gets repainted - and a property manager running dozens of doors generates that work constantly, on top of common-area and exterior refreshes across the portfolio. Through TradeWorks they book against written service levels (routine within 48 hours, urgent within 24, emergency within 4) and come back on a schedule. For a painting company, turnover work is the closest thing the trade has to recurring revenue - predictable, repeatable, and enough on its own to build a business around.

Painting contractor questions

How do painting companies get work orders through TradeWorks Select?

Apply once and get vetted. After approval, property managers and homeowners in your ZIP browse the network and either book a defined painting job on your calendar or send a quote request for a larger repaint. Defined jobs are confirmed at your price; quote requests are shared only with you and at most one other vetted painter - never blasted to five.

How much does a TradeWorks Select painting membership cost?

One flat monthly membership, priced to your ZIP's market. Across painting in Florida the median is around $110 per month, but your exact figure depends on your ZIP - enter it in the lookup on this page. There are no per-lead fees, no percentages, and no cost when a job cancels. It is introductory launch pricing and early members lock in their rate.

Is this a painting lead generation service?

No - and that is the point. Lead services sell the same homeowner's estimate request to four or five painters who then underbid each other. Select sends confirmed jobs the customer already booked, or quote requests shared only with you and one other pro. No shared blasts, no bidding to the bottom.

Do I have to bid against other painters?

No. Defined jobs are booked at your price with no competition. On larger projects, the homeowner sees a quote from you and at most one other vetted painter and selects on value and reviews - not a five-way lowest-bid auction. You quote your real number, not a race-to-the-bottom number.

What's the best Thumbtack, Angi, or HomeAdvisor alternative for painters?

TradeWorks Select. Those platforms charge per shared estimate-lead whether or not it converts and hand it to four or five painters at once. Select charges one flat membership, jobs are already booked or shared with only you and one other pro, and you keep 100% of the invoice. A cancelled order costs you nothing extra.

How can I get painting jobs without buying shared leads?

Join a network that sends confirmed work instead of selling estimate requests to everyone. With TradeWorks Select, homeowners and property managers book you directly or send a quote shared with only you and one other pro - nothing to chase, no per-lead fees, no bidding war, and one flat monthly membership as your only cost.

Do I need a license to join as a painter?

Painting isn't a state-licensed trade in Florida, so we verify your business registration and, most importantly, your insurance - plus your references and quality record. $1M/$2M general liability naming TradeWorks AI as additional insured is required, along with workers' comp if you have employees and a current W-9.

How many painting companies do you accept per ZIP code?

Seats per ZIP are deliberately limited to protect work-order volume and keep quotes shared with at most one other pro. The exact number a ZIP supports is shown in the lookup on this page. Once a ZIP is full, applications for it close until a seat opens.
