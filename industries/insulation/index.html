Yes - Florida attics get extremely hot, and proper insulation and a radiant barrier help a home hold its temperature and take load off the air conditioner, which improves comfort and efficiency. We're careful never to promise a specific dollar or percentage saving, because it depends on the home; what we can say is that a well-insulated attic makes the AC work less.
