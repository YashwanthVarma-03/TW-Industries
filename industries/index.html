Frequently Asked Questions About Industries We Serve

Why does trade-specific digital marketing matter?

Homeowners search for contractors very differently than they search for generic businesses. “HVAC near me,” “emergency plumber,” “electrician panel upgrade,” and “storm damage roofer” each require their own keywords, seasonal timing, bid strategies, and landing pages. Generic marketing agencies run the same playbook for every client. We build campaigns tailored to each trade's customer behavior, demand curve, and competitive landscape — measured in booked jobs, not clicks.

What industries does TradeWorks AI serve?

TradeWorks AI serves 31 contractor industries, including HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, painting, pest control, garage doors, cleaning, landscaping, pressure washing, gutters, handyman, moving, remodeling, and more. Every strategy is purpose-built for the unique demands of each trade.

How do you time marketing campaigns to seasonal trade demand?

HVAC peaks in summer (AC emergencies) and winter (no-heat calls). Roofing surges after storms. Pest control follows seasonal treatment cycles. Painting demand spikes in spring/summer for exterior jobs. We time your Google Ads, LSA bid adjustments, GBP posts, content calendar, and email reactivation campaigns to match your trade's real demand curve — so your budget compounds during peak demand and stays visible during off-season.

Are AI agents different for each industry?

Yes. Every AI agent is trained on trade-specific terminology, urgency rules, scheduling patterns, and service types unique to your industry. An HVAC agent understands no-heat emergencies and furnace terminology. A plumbing agent knows burst pipe protocols. A roofing agent handles storm damage call surges. A real estate agent qualifies buyers vs. sellers. No generic scripts.

What if my trade is not listed?

Contact us. If your business is adjacent to home services or another local-search-driven trade, we likely can help. Our digital marketing playbook, AI agents, and CRM consulting are adaptable to trades beyond the 31 industries listed here.
