How much does a TradeWorks Select junk removal membership cost?

One flat monthly membership, priced to your ZIP's market. Across junk removal in Florida the median is around $95 per month, but your exact figure depends on your ZIP - enter it in the lookup on this page. Why isn't the price a flat national number?

Because a ZIP's demand, home values, and cost to serve are not the same everywhere, a single national price would overcharge some companies and undercharge others. TradeWorks prices each ZIP to its own market from a statewide model, so the number is fair and defensible - and you can see it before you apply.

Is this a junk removal lead generation service?

No. Lead services sell the same homeowner's information to several companies who then underbid each other for a one-time job. Select sends confirmed work orders that a customer has already booked - one company per order, no bidding, no shared leads.

What's the best Thumbtack, Angi, or HomeAdvisor alternative for junk removal?

TradeWorks Select. Those platforms charge per shared lead whether or not it converts, and hand the same pickup to several companies at once. Select charges one flat membership, every order is already booked, and you're the only company on it. You keep 100% of the invoice, and a cancelled order costs you nothing extra.

How can I get junk removal jobs without buying shared leads?

Join a network that sends confirmed work instead of selling contact information. With TradeWorks Select, homeowners, property managers, and estates book you directly through the marketplace, so every job is already scoped and scheduled - nothing to chase, no per-lead fees, no bidding war, and one flat monthly membership as your only cost.

Do I need a license to join as a junk removal company?

Junk removal isn't state-licensed in Florida, so we verify your business registration and, most importantly, your insurance. $1M/$2M general liability naming TradeWorks AI as additional insured is required - hauling in and out of homes carries damage risk - along with workers' comp if you have employees and a current W-9. We also look for responsible disposal practices.

How many junk removal companies do you accept per ZIP code?

Seats per ZIP are deliberately limited to protect work-order volume and pricing for the members already in the network. The exact number a ZIP supports is shown in the lookup on this page. Once a ZIP is full, applications for it close until a seat opens.
