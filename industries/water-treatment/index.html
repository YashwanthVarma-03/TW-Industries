Homeowners - The classic residential mix - water softeners for the hard water that's common across Florida's limestone aquifer, whole-home filtration, reverse-osmosis drinking systems, and well-water treatment for the iron staining and sulfur odor that rural homes deal with. The recurring service on those systems is dependable revenue on top of homeowner installs.
