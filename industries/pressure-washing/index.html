How much does a TradeWorks Select pressure washing membership cost?

One flat monthly membership, priced to your ZIP's market. Why isn't the price a flat national number?

Because a ZIP's demand, home values, and cost to serve are not the same everywhere, a single national price would overcharge some companies and undercharge others. TradeWorks prices each ZIP to its own market from a statewide model, so the number is fair and defensible - and you can see it before you apply.

Is this a pressure washing lead generation service?

No. Lead services sell the same homeowner's information to several companies who then underbid each other for a one-time job. Select sends confirmed work orders that a customer has already booked - one company per order, no bidding, no shared leads.

What's the best Thumbtack, Angi, or HomeAdvisor alternative for pressure washing?

TradeWorks Select. Those platforms charge per shared lead whether or not it converts, and hand the same driveway to several companies at once. Select charges one flat membership, every order is already booked, and you're the only company on it. You keep 100% of the invoice, and a cancelled order costs you nothing extra.

How can I get pressure washing jobs without buying shared leads?

Do I need a license to join as a pressure washing company?

Pressure washing isn't a state-licensed trade in Florida, so we verify your business registration and, most importantly, your insurance. $1M/$2M general liability naming TradeWorks AI as additional insured is required - high pressure and chemicals can damage property - along with workers' comp if you have employees and a current W-9.
