Frequently Asked Questions

What makes TradeWorks AI different from a regular digital marketing agency?

Two things. First, we specialize only in home service contractors — every strategy, keyword, and campaign is built around how homeowners actually search for trades, not generic small-business marketing. Second, every service is AI-enhanced: our content marketing uses AI research plus human editorial, our GBP management uses AI-drafted posts and review responses, our GEO and AEO services are purpose-built for ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews, and our optional AI agent layer captures every lead your marketing drives 24/7. Most agencies do SEO. We do AI-enhanced SEO, GEO, AEO, and lead capture.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No. Every TradeWorks AI service is month-to-month. There are no annual contracts, no cancellation fees, and no penalties for changing your plan. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time. We believe in earning your business every month, not locking you into a commitment. If a service is not delivering value, you should be free to stop paying for it immediately.

How much does the AI agent cost per month?

The Starter AI Virtual Assistant Agent is $10 per month and includes 12 features: unlimited calls and texts, call summaries and transcripts, voicemail receptionist, AI agents for Facebook and WhatsApp, appointment management, auto schedule meetings, automated reminders, Google Calendar integration, knowledge assistance, upselling and recommendations, and customer retention. It includes a 60-day free trial with no credit card required. The Professional plan is $40 per month plus a one-time $199 initial setup fee and adds a dedicated account manager, AI agents for email and website, custom CRM integration, and more.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. The Starter plan is $10 per month with no setup fee and no additional charges. The Professional plan is $40 per month with a clearly displayed one-time $199 initial setup fee. Digital marketing services are priced individually and listed transparently on our pricing page. What you see is exactly what you pay. There are no hidden charges, no surprise invoices, and no fine print.

Can I change or cancel my plan at any time?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, add services, remove services, or cancel entirely at any time with no penalties and no cancellation fees. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. If you cancel mid-month, your service continues through the end of that billing period. We make it easy because we are confident you will want to stay.

How does the 60-day free trial work?

The 60-day free trial applies to the Starter AI Virtual Assistant Agent plan. You get full access to all 12 Starter features for 60 days at no cost and no credit card is required to start. At the end of the trial, you can continue on the Starter plan for $10 per month or upgrade to Professional. The Professional plan does not include a free trial but comes with full onboarding and a dedicated account manager included in the $199 setup fee.

Can I start with one service and add more later?

Absolutely. Most clients start with the Starter AI agent and add digital marketing services as they grow. You can add or remove any service at any time.
