Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about the TradeWorks One network, rewards, and our AI-enhanced marketing services.

How does TradeWorks One work for homeowners?

Join free, browse vetted, insured pros across 31 service categories, and see upfront pricing before you book — no quotes to chase and no bidding wars. You pick the pro you want and book directly on their calendar, and you earn service credits back on every completed job. Learn more on the Homeowners page.

How do the 3–7% rewards work?

Homeowner membership is free and earns service credits on every completed service: 3% on your first $5,000 of annual spend, 5% on spend from $5,000–$15,000, and 7% from $15,000–$25,000. Rates are marginal — each applies only to the spend within its band — and credits are non-cashable, redeemable toward any service on the menu.

What does TradeWorks One cost a property manager?

Nothing. Property managers pay a $0 platform fee — one point of contact for 31 service categories, vetted insured contractors, and 24/7 AI-assisted booking, with no markup from TradeWorks. Contractors fund the network through their membership, so you get the coordination layer at no cost. See the Property Managers page.

How do contractors get work orders through TradeWorks Select?

TradeWorks Select members receive confirmed work orders from property managers and homeowners — not shared leads, and never a bidding war. Membership is a flat monthly fee across five bands ($29–$129+, set by trade), you keep 100% of every invoice, and cancelled orders are free. Details on the TradeWorks Select page.

How is your SEO different from other digital marketing agencies?

Two things. First, we specialize only in home service contractors — every keyword, page, and campaign is built around how homeowners actually search for trades like HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, and painting. Second, every service is AI-enhanced: our content uses AI research plus human editorial, our GBP management uses AI-drafted posts and review responses, and we optimize explicitly for GEO and AEO so your business appears in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews — not just traditional Google.

What is GEO/AEO and why does it matter?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) are the practice of optimizing your website content so it is cited by AI-powered search engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews. Traditional SEO targets Google's ten blue links. GEO and AEO target the new way homeowners find contractors — by asking an AI assistant for a recommendation. If your business is not optimized for AI search, you are invisible to a rapidly growing percentage of buyers.

How does the AI agent work with my marketing?

Your digital marketing drives inbound calls, form submissions, and chats. The AI agent is the lead capture layer that ensures every one of those leads is answered 24/7, qualified against your service area and budget, and booked directly into your calendar. Without an AI agent, a portion of your marketing-driven leads go to voicemail or your competitors. With one, every marketing dollar you spend converts at a higher rate. That is why we call our approach AI-enhanced digital marketing — the marketing and AI work together, not separately.
