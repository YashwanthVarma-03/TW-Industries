Overview

What Is Automation & Workflow Integration?

TradeWorks AI connects to the automation platforms your business relies on, so data flows between your tools without manual effort. Every lead, appointment, and customer interaction can trigger the next step automatically.

Zapier

Connect TradeWorks AI to over 5,000 apps with zero code. Zapier triggers fire when your AI agent captures a new lead, books an appointment, or logs a missed call — automatically pushing that data to your CRM, email platform, spreadsheet, or any other tool in your stack. Setup takes minutes, not days.

Make (Integromat)

For contractors who need more complex, multi-step workflows, Make provides a visual drag-and-drop builder with conditional logic, branching paths, and error handling. Route leads differently based on service type, schedule follow-ups based on job value, or sync data across ServiceTitan, Jobber, and your accounting software in a single workflow.

Custom Webhooks & API

For maximum control, TradeWorks AI exposes webhooks and a REST API that your development team — or our consulting team — can use to build custom integrations. Push real-time event data to any system, build custom dashboards, or connect to proprietary tools that Zapier and Make do not support.

AI Agents + Automation

Every event your AI agent handles — a new lead captured, an appointment booked, a missed call logged, or an emergency triaged — can trigger an automated workflow that saves your team hours of manual work every week.

When a new lead comes in, automation can instantly add that contact to your ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro CRM, send a Slack notification to your dispatcher, create a follow-up task in your project management tool, and log the interaction in a shared Google Sheet — all before anyone on your team lifts a finger.

Common AI agent automation workflows include:

  • Auto-add new leads to your CRM with full call context
  • Send Slack or Microsoft Teams notifications for high-value opportunities
  • Create invoices or estimates in QuickBooks when jobs are booked
  • Update shared spreadsheets with real-time lead and appointment data
  • Trigger email nurture sequences for leads that are not ready to book

Marketing + Automation

Which Automation Platform Is Right for You?

TradeWorks AI supports all three approaches. Choose based on your team's needs, technical comfort, and workflow complexity.

Easiest

Zapier

  • 5,000+ supported apps
  • No-code setup in minutes
  • Best for simple, linear workflows
  • From $19.99/mo (Zapier pricing)

Recommended for contractors who want quick wins: auto-add leads to your CRM, send notifications, update spreadsheets. If your workflow is “when X happens, do Y,” Zapier is the fastest path.

Most Flexible

Make (Integromat)

  • Visual drag-and-drop workflow builder
  • Conditional logic & branching
  • Best for multi-step, complex workflows
  • From $9/mo (Make pricing)

Recommended for contractors with complex operations: route leads by service type, build multi-step onboarding sequences, sync data across three or more platforms with error handling and retry logic.

Most Powerful

Custom Webhooks & API

  • Real-time event data via webhooks
  • Full REST API access
  • Best for enterprise & proprietary systems
  • Included with consulting engagement

Recommended for larger contractors or franchises with proprietary systems, custom dashboards, or compliance requirements that Zapier and Make cannot meet. Our consulting team builds and maintains these integrations for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there an extra cost for automation & workflow integration?

The Professional plan at $40 per month includes native integrations with Zapier, Make, and webhook support. Zapier and Make subscriptions are billed separately at their own pricing. Custom webhook consulting starts at $199 for up to 5 workflows. Complex automation projects involving multiple platforms or advanced logic start at $499. There are no hidden fees and no long-term contracts on any plan.

Do I need technical knowledge to set up automations?

No. TradeWorks AI configures everything for you. During onboarding, we identify the workflows that will save your team the most time, build and test them, and hand you a system that runs on autopilot. If you ever want to modify a workflow or add a new one, our consulting team handles that too. You never need to touch a line of code or configure a single Zap yourself.

Which automation platform do you recommend?

It depends on your needs. Zapier is the best choice for simplicity and quick setup — it connects to over 5,000 apps and works well for straightforward “when X happens, do Y” workflows. Make (formerly Integromat) is ideal for complex visual workflows with conditional logic, branching, and error handling. Custom webhooks via our API are the most powerful option for enterprise-grade automation but require development work, which our consulting team handles. Most contractors start with Zapier and graduate to Make or custom webhooks as their operations grow.

Can I automate workflows between TradeWorks AI and my CRM?

Yes. TradeWorks AI supports direct integrations with ServiceTitan, Jobber, and Housecall Pro, as well as automation-platform connections to HubSpot, Salesforce, and virtually any CRM that supports Zapier, Make, or webhooks. Common workflows include auto-creating CRM contacts from new leads, syncing appointment data, triggering follow-up sequences, and updating job statuses across systems.
