Platform Overview

What Is Zoho?

Zoho is an affordable, full-featured CRM suite that combines customer relationship management, email marketing, project management, and business automation into one integrated ecosystem. It gives growing businesses enterprise-level tools without enterprise-level pricing.

Zoho is best for multi-service contractors who want CRM, email, and projects in one ecosystem without paying for separate tools. If your business manages multiple service lines, needs email campaigns alongside deal tracking, and wants a single platform that handles sales, support, and project management, Zoho delivers exceptional value at a fraction of the cost of Salesforce or HubSpot.

TradeWorks AI integrates directly with Zoho CRM's API, so your AI agent can create contacts, push deals into your pipeline, log conversation transcripts on the activity timeline, and trigger Blueprint workflows without anyone touching a keyboard.

Honest note: For trade-specific field service management features like dispatching, pricebooks, and technician scheduling, consider ServiceTitan, Jobber, or Housecall Pro. Zoho excels as a sales and business management CRM but does not include FSM features natively. We help you choose the right platform during your free audit — we do not push one over another.

AI + Zoho

How AI Agents Connect to Zoho

Every interaction your AI agent handles flows directly into Zoho CRM in real time. No copy-pasting, no double entry, no leads slipping through the cracks.

1

Lead → Zoho Contact

When your AI agent captures a new lead via phone, text, or web chat, it automatically creates a Contact record in Zoho CRM with the caller's name, phone number, email, company, and service request details. Your sales team sees the new contact instantly on their dashboard.

2

Deal → Pipeline

The AI agent creates a Deal in your Zoho pipeline with the correct stage, estimated value, and close date. As the conversation progresses, the deal stage updates automatically — from qualification to proposal to closed-won — giving your team real-time pipeline visibility.

3

Activity → Timeline

Every conversation — phone calls, text messages, web chat sessions — is logged as an Activity on the Contact and Deal records. Your team has full context for every interaction on Zoho's timeline view. Combined with communication tools, you get a complete customer history.

4

Workflow → Blueprint Trigger

When the AI agent updates a deal stage or creates a new record, it can trigger Zoho Blueprint workflows automatically. Your existing automation — email sequences, task assignments, approval processes, and multi-step workflows — activates without any manual intervention.

Marketing Data Flowing Into Zoho

Your digital marketing campaigns generate leads from Google Ads, organic search, social media, and your website. TradeWorks AI ensures every one of those leads automatically populates in Zoho CRM — with full attribution data so you know exactly which campaign drove each contact.

When a prospect submits a form on your website, the AI agent captures the lead, creates the Contact in Zoho, opens a Deal in your pipeline, and logs the full conversation context on the timeline. Your sales team picks up a warm lead with complete context instead of a cold record with just a name and phone number.

Key marketing-to-Zoho data flows:

  • Google Ads and SEO leads auto-populate as new Zoho Contacts with campaign source tags
  • Conversion tracking feeds back into your ad platforms for bid optimization
  • Closed-won deals attribute revenue to the originating marketing campaign
  • Zoho Campaigns integration lets you nurture leads captured by the AI agent with automated email sequences
  • Missed-call leads are captured by the AI agent and added to Zoho before the prospect calls a competitor

From kickoff to go-live in 10 business days. Here is exactly what happens and when.

Discovery — Day 1

We review your Zoho CRM instance, current pipeline and Blueprint configuration, AI agent requirements, and business goals. We identify which data needs to flow between systems and map out your ideal automation workflows.

API Connection — Day 2–3
