Platform Overview

What Is Salesforce?

Salesforce is the world's leading enterprise CRM platform, offering powerful pipeline management, advanced reporting, custom automation, and a vast ecosystem of integrations. It gives businesses complete visibility into their sales process from first contact to closed deal.

Salesforce is best for larger operations with complex sales processes that need advanced pipeline management, custom reporting dashboards, multi-step automation workflows, and deep integration with other enterprise tools. If your sales team manages hundreds of leads per month and needs granular control over every stage of the pipeline, Salesforce is the industry standard.

TradeWorks AI integrates directly with Salesforce's API, so your AI agent can create contacts, push deals into your pipeline, log conversation transcripts as activities, and trigger automation workflows without anyone touching a keyboard.

Honest note: For trade-specific field service management features like dispatching, pricebooks, and technician scheduling, consider ServiceTitan, Jobber, or Housecall Pro. Salesforce excels as a sales and pipeline CRM but does not include FSM features natively. We help you choose the right platform during your free audit — we do not push one over another.

AI + Salesforce

How AI Agents Connect to Salesforce

Every interaction your AI agent handles flows directly into Salesforce in real time. No copy-pasting, no double entry, no leads slipping through the cracks.

1

Lead → Salesforce Contact

When your AI agent captures a new lead via phone, text, or web chat, it automatically creates a Contact record in Salesforce with the caller's name, phone number, email, company, and service request details. Your sales team sees the new contact instantly on their dashboard.

2

Deal → Pipeline

The AI agent creates an Opportunity in your Salesforce pipeline with the correct deal stage, estimated value, and close date. As the conversation progresses, the deal stage updates automatically — from qualification to proposal to closed-won — giving your team real-time pipeline visibility.

3

Notes → Activity Log

Every conversation — phone calls, text messages, web chat sessions — is logged as an Activity on the Contact and Opportunity records. Your sales team has full context for every interaction without asking the customer to repeat themselves. Combined with communication tools, you get a complete customer timeline.

4

Conversion → Revenue Attribution

When a deal closes, the revenue is attributed back to the original marketing source — Google Ads, organic search, referral, or any other channel. Your marketing team can finally see true ROI by campaign, not just cost-per-lead, but actual revenue generated.

Marketing Data Flowing Into Salesforce

Your digital marketing campaigns generate leads from Google Ads, organic search, social media, and your website. TradeWorks AI ensures every one of those leads automatically populates in Salesforce — with full attribution data so you know exactly which campaign drove each contact.

When a prospect submits a form on your website, the AI agent captures the lead, creates the Contact in Salesforce, opens an Opportunity in your pipeline, and logs the full conversation context as an Activity. Your sales team picks up a warm lead with complete context instead of a cold record with just a name and phone number.

Key marketing-to-Salesforce data flows:

  • Google Ads and SEO leads auto-populate as new Salesforce Contacts with campaign source tags
  • Conversion tracking feeds back into your ad platforms for bid optimization
  • Closed-won deals attribute revenue to the originating marketing campaign
  • Marketing source data is tagged on every Contact and Opportunity for lifetime ROI tracking
  • Missed-call leads are captured by the AI agent and added to Salesforce before the prospect calls a competitor
