Platform Overview

What Is ServiceTitan?

ServiceTitan is the leading enterprise field service management platform built for large HVAC, plumbing, and electrical operations. It combines advanced dispatching, deep pricebook management, membership programs, marketing attribution, and enterprise-level reporting into a single platform designed for scale.

ServiceTitan is best for contractors with 10 or more technicians who need enterprise-level dispatch, advanced pricebook capabilities, membership management, and deep reporting. If your operation is growing fast and you need a platform that can scale with you, ServiceTitan is the industry standard.

TradeWorks AI integrates directly with ServiceTitan's API, so your AI agent can create leads, book calls to the dispatch board, reference pricebook items for estimates, and sync customer records without your CSRs ever touching a keyboard.

Honest note: For growing teams with 3 to 15 technicians who do not yet need enterprise-level features, consider Jobber — it offers excellent quoting and scheduling at a lower price point. For simplicity and ease of use with 1 to 10 technicians, consider Housecall Pro. We help you choose the right platform during your free audit — we do not push one over another.

AI + ServiceTitan

How AI Agents Connect to ServiceTitan

Every interaction your AI agent handles flows directly into ServiceTitan in real time. No copy-pasting, no double entry, no leads slipping through the cracks.

1

Lead Created

When your AI agent captures a new lead via phone, text, or web chat, it automatically creates a lead record in ServiceTitan with the caller's name, phone number, address, and service request details. Your CSR team sees the new lead instantly on their dashboard.

2

Call Booked to Dispatch

The AI agent checks technician availability and books the call directly onto ServiceTitan's dispatch board with the correct job type, time window, and technician assignment. The dispatcher sees the booked call immediately — no manual re-entry required.

3

Pricebook Estimate

For leads that need a quote before booking, the AI agent can reference your ServiceTitan pricebook to provide accurate estimates during the conversation. When the customer approves, the estimate converts into a booked call on your dispatch board automatically.

4

Customer Record Synced

Every interaction — call notes, service history, contact details, and communication preferences — is synced to the customer record in ServiceTitan. Your technicians arrive at the job with full context, and your office team has a complete communication history for every customer.

Marketing Data Flowing Into ServiceTitan

Your digital marketing campaigns generate leads from Google Ads, organic search, social media, and your website. TradeWorks AI ensures every one of those leads automatically populates in ServiceTitan — with full attribution data so you know exactly which campaign drove each customer.

When a homeowner submits a form on your website, the AI agent captures the lead, creates the customer in ServiceTitan, and can book the call before your CSR team even sees the notification. ServiceTitan's built-in marketing scorecard then tracks that lead from first touch to completed job and revenue.

Key marketing-to-ServiceTitan data flows:

  • Google Ads and SEO leads auto-populate as new ServiceTitan leads with campaign source tags

  • ServiceTitan's marketing scorecard tracks cost-per-lead and revenue-per-campaign automatically
  • Marketing source data is tagged on every customer record for lifetime ROI tracking
  • Missed-call leads are captured by the AI agent and added to ServiceTitan before the customer calls a competitor

Integration Setup Timeline

From kickoff to go-live in 10 business days. Here is exactly what happens and when.

1

Discovery — Day 1

We review your ServiceTitan account, current workflows, AI agent requirements, and business goals. We identify which data needs to flow between systems and map out your ideal dispatch and booking automation workflows.

2

API Connection — Day 2–3

Our team connects TradeWorks AI to your ServiceTitan account via the ServiceTitan Open API. We configure authentication, test the connection, and verify that data can flow in both directions securely.

3

Field Mapping — Day 4–5

We map your TradeWorks AI data fields to the corresponding ServiceTitan fields — customer names, phone numbers, addresses, job types, pricebook items, dispatch zones, and any custom fields your business requires. Nothing gets lost in translation.

Testing — Day 6–8

We run end-to-end tests with your team: simulated leads, booked calls on the dispatch board, pricebook estimate workflows, and customer record syncs. We verify every data point lands correctly in ServiceTitan and fix any issues before going live.

Go-Live — Day 9–10

Your integration goes live with full monitoring. We watch the first batch of real leads flow through, confirm everything is working as expected, and provide your team with a quick-reference guide. Ongoing support is included on all plans.
