Reach customers on the world's most popular messaging app. Your AI agent responds to WhatsApp inquiries instantly — answering questions, qualifying leads, and booking appointments 24/7. Included on the Starter plan at $10/mo with unlimited messages.

Facebook Messenger

Capture leads directly from your Facebook business page. When a homeowner sends a message through Messenger, your AI agent responds in seconds with accurate service information, availability, and booking options. Included on the Starter plan with no per-message fees.

SMS (Native)

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which channels are included on the Starter plan?

Facebook, WhatsApp, SMS, and phone are all included on the Starter plan at $10 per month. All channels are unlimited — no per-message fees, no overage charges. Your AI agent handles every conversation across all four channels from day one.

Which channels require Professional?

Email and website chat require the Professional plan at $40 per month. Professional also includes all Starter channels (Facebook, WhatsApp, SMS, and phone) plus advanced automation and workflow capabilities. If your business relies heavily on email communication or wants a live chat widget on your website, Professional is the right choice.

Is there a per-message fee?

No. All communication channels are unlimited on both the Starter and Professional plans. There are no per-message fees, no overage charges, and no hidden costs. Whether your AI agent sends 100 or 10,000 messages in a month, the price stays the same.

Is there an extra cost for communication integration?

No extra cost. The Starter plan at $10 per month includes Facebook, WhatsApp, and SMS. The Professional plan at $40 per month adds email and website chat. Custom workflow automation consulting starts at $199 for businesses that need advanced routing, templates, or multi-channel escalation workflows. There are no hidden fees and no long-term contracts on any plan.
