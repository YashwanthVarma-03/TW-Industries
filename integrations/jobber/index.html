Platform Overview

What Is Jobber?

Jobber is a field service management platform designed for home service businesses. It combines quoting, scheduling, invoicing, payments, and client communication into one system that your team can access from the office or the field.

Jobber is best for growing teams with 3 to 15 technicians who need an intuitive interface with strong quoting workflows, client approval processes, and a polished client hub. If your team sends a lot of quotes and wants customers to approve them online with one click, Jobber is an excellent choice.

TradeWorks AI integrates directly with Jobber's API, so your AI agent can create customers, generate quotes, book jobs, and trigger follow-up automations without your team ever logging in to enter data manually.

Honest note: For enterprise operations with 10 or more technicians that need advanced dispatching, membership management, and deep reporting, ServiceTitan may be a better fit. For all-in-one simplicity with built-in marketing tools, consider Housecall Pro. We help you choose the right platform during your free audit — we do not push one over another.

AI + Jobber

How AI Agents Connect to Jobber

Every interaction your AI agent handles flows directly into Jobber in real time. No copy-pasting, no double entry, no leads slipping through the cracks.

1

New Lead → Creates Customer in Jobber

When your AI agent captures a new lead via phone, text, or web chat, it automatically creates a customer record in Jobber with the caller's name, phone number, address, and service request details. Your team sees the new customer instantly in their Jobber dashboard.

2

Appointment → Creates Job With Service Details

The AI agent checks availability and books the appointment directly in Jobber, creating a job with the correct service type, technician assignment, and time slot. The customer receives a confirmation via Jobber's built-in notification system.

3

Quote Request → Generates Jobber Quote

For leads that need pricing before committing, the AI agent generates and sends a quote through Jobber. Customers receive a professional quote they can approve with one tap through Jobber's client hub, and the approved job flows right into your schedule.

4

Follow-Up → Triggers Automated Sequence

After the initial interaction, the AI agent triggers automated follow-up sequences — appointment reminders, post-job review requests, and unapproved quote nudges. Combined with workflow automation, you can build multi-step sequences that run on autopilot.
