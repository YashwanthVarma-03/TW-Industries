Platform Overview

What Is QuickBooks?

QuickBooks is the leading accounting and invoicing platform for small and mid-sized businesses. It handles invoicing, payment tracking, expense management, payroll, and financial reporting — giving contractors a complete picture of their business finances.

QuickBooks is best for contractors who need invoice sync, payment tracking, and financial reporting tied directly to the jobs their team completes in the field. When connected to your CRM, QuickBooks becomes the financial backbone of your operation — every completed job flows into an invoice, every payment is recorded, and every dollar is tracked for tax season.

TradeWorks AI connects to QuickBooks through your CRM. Your AI agent captures leads and creates jobs in your field service platform, and your CRM's native QuickBooks integration handles the financial sync — invoices, payments, and customer records all flow downstream automatically.

Honest note: QuickBooks is an accounting platform, not a field service management tool. You still need a CRM like Housecall Pro, Jobber, or ServiceTitan to handle scheduling, dispatching, and job management. QuickBooks sits downstream of your CRM to handle the financial side. AI + QuickBooks

How AI Agents Connect to QuickBooks

Your AI agent captures leads and books jobs in your CRM. Your CRM syncs completed jobs and invoices to QuickBooks. The result is a closed financial loop with no manual data entry at any step.

1

Invoice Created

When your AI agent books a job and your technician completes it, the invoice is generated in your CRM and automatically synced to QuickBooks. Customer details, line items, service descriptions, and totals all flow through without anyone typing a single number.

2

Payment Received

When a customer pays through your CRM's payment processing, QuickBooks records the payment automatically. Your accounts receivable stays current, and your team never has to reconcile payments manually between systems.

3

Revenue Tracked

Every completed job contributes to your revenue reporting in QuickBooks. Track revenue by service type, technician, month, or marketing source — all powered by the data your AI agent captured at the point of first contact.

4

Tax Reports

With clean, complete financial data flowing into QuickBooks automatically, your tax reporting is accurate from day one. No more scrambling at year-end to reconcile invoices, chase missing payments, or reconstruct job records from memory. Your CRM consultant ensures the data flows correctly from the start.
