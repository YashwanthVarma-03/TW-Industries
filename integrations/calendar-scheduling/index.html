Overview

What Is Calendar & Scheduling Integration?

TradeWorks AI connects directly to the calendar platforms your business already uses, so your AI agent can check availability, book appointments, send confirmations, and handle rescheduling — all without human intervention.

Google Calendar

The most popular calendar for small and mid-size trade businesses. TradeWorks AI reads and writes to Google Calendar in real time, so your AI agent always knows which slots are open. Included on all plans — no extra cost, no extra configuration.

Microsoft Outlook

For contractors running on Microsoft 365, TradeWorks AI syncs with Outlook calendars across your entire organization. Multi-technician availability, shared calendars, and recurring appointment support are all built in. Available on the Professional plan.

Calendly

If your business uses Calendly scheduling links for customer self-booking, TradeWorks AI integrates directly so your AI agent and your Calendly page share the same availability pool. No more conflicting bookings between phone calls and online scheduling. Available on the Professional plan.

iCal

For contractors in the Apple ecosystem, TradeWorks AI supports iCal via CalDAV. Appointments booked by your AI agent appear instantly on your iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Ideal for owner-operators who manage their schedule from a single device. Available on the Professional plan.

AI Agents + Calendar Scheduling

When a homeowner calls your business, your AI agent does not just answer the phone — it checks your calendar in real time, finds the next available slot, and books the appointment on the spot. The customer gets an instant confirmation via text or email, and your technician sees the new job on their calendar within seconds.

If a customer needs to reschedule, the AI agent handles that too. It checks updated availability, moves the appointment, notifies the technician, and sends the customer a new confirmation. No calls to your office. No sticky notes. No forgotten changes.

  • Instant appointment booking with customer confirmation via SMS or email

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the AI agent book directly into my calendar?

Yes. The AI agent checks your real-time availability and instantly books the appointment into your calendar. There is no delay and no manual step. Google Calendar is supported on all plans including Starter at $10 per month. Outlook, Calendly, and iCal integrations are available on the Professional plan at $40 per month.

Can I set buffer time between appointments?

Yes. Buffer time is fully configurable per technician and per service type. For example, you can set 30 minutes of travel time between HVAC installations but only 15 minutes between diagnostic calls. Our consulting team configures these rules during onboarding so your schedule stays realistic and your technicians are never double-booked.

What happens if two people try to book the same slot?

The AI agent checks real-time availability before confirming any booking. If a slot is taken between the time a caller asks about it and the time the booking is confirmed, the agent automatically offers the next available slot. Double-bookings are not possible.

Is there an extra cost for calendar integration?

Google Calendar integration is included on the Starter plan at $10 per month and the Professional plan at $40 per month. Outlook, Calendly, and iCal integrations are included on the Professional plan at no additional charge. Custom calendar configuration consulting starts at $199 for rules and buffer setup, or $499 for a full multi-technician, multi-location scheduling build. There are no hidden fees and no long-term contracts.
