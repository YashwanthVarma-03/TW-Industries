Platform Overview

What Is Housecall Pro?

Housecall Pro is an all-in-one field service management platform built for home service contractors. It combines scheduling, dispatching, invoicing, payments, and customer communication into a single app that your team can access from the office or the field.

Housecall Pro is best for contractors with 1 to 10 technicians who want a simple, intuitive platform with built-in marketing tools like automated review requests, email campaigns, and an online booking page. If your team values ease of use over deep customization, Housecall Pro is an excellent choice.

TradeWorks AI integrates directly with Housecall Pro's API, so your AI agent can create customers, book jobs, send estimates, and trigger follow-up automations without your team ever logging in to enter data manually.

Honest note: If you have 10 or more technicians and need enterprise-level dispatch, reporting, and membership management, ServiceTitan may be a better fit. If you need more robust quoting and client approval workflows, consider Jobber. We help you choose the right platform during your free audit — we do not push one over another.

AI + Housecall Pro

How AI Agents Connect to Housecall Pro

Every interaction your AI agent handles flows directly into Housecall Pro in real time. No copy-pasting, no double entry, no leads slipping through the cracks.

1

New Lead Captured

When your AI agent captures a new lead via phone, text, or web chat, it automatically creates a customer record in Housecall Pro with the caller's name, phone number, address, and service request details. Your team sees the new customer instantly.

2

Appointment Booked

The AI agent checks availability and books the appointment directly in Housecall Pro, creating a job with the correct service type, technician assignment, and time slot. The customer receives a confirmation via HCP's built-in notification system.

3

Follow-Up Triggered

After the initial interaction, the AI agent can trigger Housecall Pro's built-in automation — sending appointment reminders, post-job review requests, and follow-up emails. Combined with workflow automation, you can build multi-step sequences that run on autopilot.

4

Estimate Sent

For leads that need a quote before booking, the AI agent can generate and send an estimate through Housecall Pro's online booking system. Customers receive a professional estimate they can approve with one tap, and the approved job flows right into your schedule.
